RSI (14)

Above 70 = overbought, below 30 = oversold. A momentum hint, not a command.

Real vs nominal returns

At 6% inflation, a 12% return is ~5.7% in real purchasing power. Always model inflation.

Monte Carlo

Instead of one guess, random yearly returns produce a range of outcomes — showing uncertainty honestly.

Volatility drag

A 50% loss needs a 100% gain to recover. Big swings cost more than they appear.

Step-up SIP

Raising contributions with income beats chasing returns. Easiest way to grow a corpus.
